How to race

Change lane: W/S or ↑/↓ · Pedal: hold SPACE
Your kart's top speed follows the REAL wheel RPM. Dodge rocks, slugs and surfacing moles (3 hearts). Pearl seeds are +10 pts and a small boost.

💤 When Squeektoshi naps, your engine dies — but the ghost is a recording and keeps racing! Hold SPACE to pedal at low speed until he wakes.
